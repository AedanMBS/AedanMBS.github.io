~/project

What I'm building outside of coursework.

Purple Meridian Index

in development

Hiring in cybersecurity is still mostly resume-based — years of experience, listed certifications, keyword matches. I wanted to try building something that ranks candidates by demonstrated skill instead, closer to how competitive rating systems already work in chess and online games.

Purple Meridian Index is a candidate-ranking platform built around a Glicko-2 rating model — the same family of statistical rating systems used to rank chess and esports players — applied to cybersecurity skill rather than match outcomes. Internally, I call the resulting score an IX point rating.

Right now I have working HTML prototypes built, and I'm moving the project into its production form. It's very much a work in progress, and it's where I'm doing most of my hands-on full-stack and applied-security learning outside of class.

TypeScript Next.js Supabase WSL2 / Linux Glicko-2
